Cookie Policy

This page explains how the current version of the Smerfiki Nursery website uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. Does the website use cookies?

The current version of the website does not use analytics, advertising or marketing cookies and does not run tracking tools that require consent. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, embedded Facebook plugins, YouTube embeds or embedded Google Maps.

2. Strictly necessary technical technologies

The hosting or infrastructure provider may use technical mechanisms that are necessary to operate the website securely, prevent abuse or process a user request. Where such mechanisms are strictly necessary to provide the service, they do not require consent. We do not use them for profiling or advertising.

3. External links

The website contains ordinary links to services such as Facebook, Google Maps and the EU funding website. Merely viewing the Smerfiki website does not load embedded content from those services. After you click an external link, you leave our website and the external service may use its own cookies under its own policy.

4. Why there is no consent banner

Because the website currently does not use cookies or similar technologies that require consent, we do not display an “Accept / Reject” cookie banner. If analytics, advertising pixels or embedded third-party content requiring consent are added later, a consent mechanism should be implemented before those technologies are loaded.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website or the services it uses change. Last updated: 13 October 2026.

For information about personal data processing, see our Privacy Policy.
