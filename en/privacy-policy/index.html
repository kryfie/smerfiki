Privacy Policy

This page explains how personal data is processed when you use the Smerfiki Nursery website and contact forms.

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data is Firma Handlowo-Usługowa Wojciech Sekuła, Wójta Radtkego 36/40, 81-355 Gdynia, Poland. You can contact the controller at zlobeksmerfiki@wp.pl or by phone: 507 105 269 / 696 416 666.

2. What data we process

When you use a contact form we may process your name, phone number, e-mail address, selected nursery location and the content of your message. The hosting provider may also process technical data necessary to operate and secure the website, such as IP address and server logs. Please do not send a child’s PESEL number, medical information or other sensitive data through the website form.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

We process contact-form data to respond to your enquiry and, where the enquiry concerns enrolment or a service, to take steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). In other contact cases and for securing the website or establishing, pursuing or defending claims, processing may be based on the controller’s legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use contact-form data for marketing unless a separate legal basis is provided.

4. Recipients and hosting

Data may be made available to entities that support us in providing IT, hosting, e-mail, legal or accounting services, only to the extent necessary. The website and its forms are hosted using Netlify. Netlify stores form submissions on its infrastructure and acts as a service provider/processor for customer content. Netlify states that cross-border transfers are protected using applicable legal transfer mechanisms, including Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

5. Retention

Enquiries that do not lead to an agreement are generally retained for no longer than 12 months after the enquiry is closed, unless a longer period is necessary to establish, pursue or defend claims. If the enquiry leads to an agreement, data may be retained for the period required by tax, accounting and limitation rules. Form submissions stored in Netlify should be reviewed and deleted regularly in line with this retention rule.

6. Your rights

Depending on the legal basis and circumstances, you have the right to request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

7. Voluntary provision of data

Providing data in the form is voluntary, but the contact details marked as required are necessary for us to respond to your enquiry.

8. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you solely by automated means and we do not profile users of the contact forms.

9. Cookies and external links

The current version of the website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. We do not embed social media or maps on the page. If you click an external link, for example Facebook, Google Maps or the EU funding website, the external service will process data according to its own privacy rules.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the website, forms or service providers change. Last updated: 13 October 2026.
